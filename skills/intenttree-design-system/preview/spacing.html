Design System · Spacing & atmosphere

Spacing, radius, shadow, motion

4pt grid · soft elevation · calm motion

Layout sits on a 4pt grid. Radii are soft and consistent — the whole product has rounded corners, never sharp. Elevation is layered with low-opacity shadow plus subtle blur, never a hard drop shadow. Motion orients the user — every transition has a spatial purpose, never decoration.
