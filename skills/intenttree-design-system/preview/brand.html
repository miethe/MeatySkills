Mascot & delight

A small pixel tree. Surfaces in the sidebar after meaningful completions. Never in tables, dashboards, or dense data.

"You found me. Keep building."

The tree grows leaves after meaningful completions — never on routine task ticks. A single tooltip on long-term return; no XP bars, no levels, no popups.

Voice rules

  • Verbs first on actionable surfaces. "Execute Next", "Group these tasks?", "Resolve to continue".
  • Concise & operational. Status before sentiment. "Two tasks blocked" beats "Yikes — we have a problem!"
  • Recommend, don't decree. "Recommended: Hybrid. AI scaffolds, you refine."
  • No XP. No streaks. No emoji confetti. Delight is restrained: a small sparkle on completion, a leaf growing on the mascot.
  • Shepherd is grounded. The assistant clarifies, never performs. No exclamation points.
