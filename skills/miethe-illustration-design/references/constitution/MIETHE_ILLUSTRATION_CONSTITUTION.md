# Miethe Illustration Constitution

## 1. Core purpose
Create media that helps the Miethe family—and audiences like them—feel that ordinary life, learning, making, and loving can become part of **a brighter tomorrow**.

This constitution is the underlying visual soul shared across all ten dialects.

## 2. The soul of the system
This design language assumes:
- curiosity is good
- kindness is strength
- family togetherness is formative
- children are full people, not props
- learning is part of love
- noticing is a creative act
- technology should serve human flourishing
- the future should be built, not merely consumed

## 3. Canonical family cast

- imaginative father
- emotionally present

### Wife / Mom
- grounding co-anchor

## 4. Non-negotiable principles
- emotionally safe
- child-honoring
- warm and hopeful
- specific rather than generic
- beautiful but useful
- clear enough for print and digital use
- capable of scaling from books to apps to posters

## 5. Shared visual primitives
### Motifs
- rivers and water
- mountains and trees
- stars and moonlight
- notebooks, maps, and journals
- cameras and noticing tools
- books and literacy
- leaves, flowers, terrariums, gardens
- tools, blocks, gears, and workshop objects
- lanterns and warm points of light
- signs, hand labels, and tiny wisdom notes

### Material language
Across dialects, the work tends to feel:
- painterly or tactile
- softly lit
- hand-touched
- textured rather than slick
- human rather than corporate

### Light logic
- warm light implies care, home, presence, or understanding
- cooler light implies wonder, night, reflection, or contemplation
- light sources are often emotional anchors

### Color logic
Use calm base neutrals with one emotional accent family per piece.
Common constitutional colors:
- paper cream
- atlas / moon blue
- sage / moss green
- blush pink
- starlight gold
- warm timber / hearth tones
- lavender mist

### Typography voice
- literary serif for major titles
- friendly small caps for labels and UI/system moments
- handwritten annotations for intimacy and human touch

## 6. Narrative rules
- small moments matter
- ordinary life becomes meaningful
- learning is adventure
- home is a launch point
- curiosity becomes action
- the future is cultivated

## 7. Media types the constitution supports
- children’s books
- chapter spreads
- storyboards and moodboards
- posters and wall prints
- educational cards and activity sheets
- app graphics and interface concepts
- family journals and keepsakes
- agent-generated experiments and derivative worlds

## 8. Blending rules
A style blend must preserve the constitution. Recommended formula:
- one primary dialect
- one secondary dialect
- constitution stays dominant

Examples:
- Rivermind + Piano Rain = bedtime music-poetry
- Quiet Kyoto + Montessori Meadow = calm language learning for young kids
- Maker Constellation + Wonder Workshop = hands-on STEM play
- Lenslight + Mosaic Story Atlas = travel journaling and memory capture

## 9. Anti-patterns
Avoid by default:
- cynical irony
- mean-spirited humor
- chaotic overstimulation
- grim dystopia
- cold corporate slickness
- generic stock-family blandness
- adults overshadowing children’s agency
