# Lenslight Field Journal — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Attention is the artistic act. Observation turns everyday life into story.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The noticing style: photography, observation, and the extraordinary hidden in the ordinary.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- field journals
- nature graphics
- photo-rich family books
- educational observation prompts
- science/nature apps


## Core metaphors

- camera frames
- field notes
- pressed plants
- specimen pages
- backpacks
- trail treasures


## Material language

- watercolor plus journal ephemera
- handwriting overlays
- specimen sheet composition
- vintage camera motifs


## Palette cues

- forest green
- paper tan
- sunset gold
- lake blue
- fern green
- ink brown


## Composition rules

- mix scene illustration with labeled notes
- foreground collected objects
- clear sense of discovery
- keep observational detail high but gentle


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- nature journaling
- field guides
- photo essays
- botanical illustration
- the user's photography and curiosity


## Prompt emphasis
Make noticing feel sacred, intimate, and quietly exciting.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- overprocessed tech UI look
- aggressive adventure macho energy
- clutter without hierarchy


## Good blend partners

- 02 Blue Ridge Campfire Quilt
- 05 Montessori Meadow Cut-Paper
- 04 Maker Constellation


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?
