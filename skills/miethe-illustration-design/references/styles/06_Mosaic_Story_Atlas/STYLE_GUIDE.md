# Mosaic Story Atlas — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Experience becomes permanent artifact. Small pieces join into a durable family story.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The travel-and-memory style: places, culture, and lasting shared narrative.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- travel journals
- place-based storybooks
- family memory maps
- culture-rich graphics
- keepsakes


## Core metaphors

- mosaic tesserae
- maps
- coastal towns
- compass roses
- journals
- collected moments


## Material language

- mosaic texture
- sunlit painted stone
- map linework
- Mediterranean palette harmonies


## Palette cues

- sea blue
- terracotta
- sun cream
- olive green
- coral pink
- tile teal


## Composition rules

- build scenes from many small shape relationships
- embrace pattern and place identity
- keep the whole image cohesive despite many parts
- use decorative borders or map elements sparingly


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- Mediterranean mosaics
- travel posters
- family atlas imagery
- storybook cartography
- memory-keeping design


## Prompt emphasis
Capture place, belonging, and the beauty of many moments becoming one story.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- generic tourism clichés
- overly photoreal realism
- monochrome flatness
- crowded unreadable tiling


## Good blend partners

- 07 Lenslight Field Journal
- 02 Blue Ridge Campfire Quilt
- 10 Hearthlight Future Garden


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Mosaic Story Atlas within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.

Narrative goal: [insert goal].
Use motifs such as: mosaic tesserae, maps, coastal towns, compass roses, journals.
Visual language should emphasize: mosaic texture, sunlit painted stone, map linework.
```
