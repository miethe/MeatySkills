# Quiet Kyoto Playbook — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Learning becomes ritual. Meaning emerges through restraint, seasonality, and gentle repetition.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The mindful learning style: Japanese language, quiet study, seasonality, and deliberate noticing.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- Japanese learning materials
- vocabulary cards
- quiet educational apps
- culture-rich stories
- mindful routines


## Core metaphors

- paper
- seasons
- paths
- torii and temple silhouettes
- tea-like calm
- small steps


## Material language

- soft ink lines
- washy watercolor
- paper grain
- subtle collage
- minimal diagram notes


## Palette cues

- blush pink
- ink navy
- rice paper cream
- spring leaf
- mist grey
- warm wood


## Composition rules

- respect negative space
- limit visual noise
- use asymmetry thoughtfully
- anchor each spread with one focal teaching moment
- gentle margins and whitespace


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- Japanese picture-book restraint
- ukiyo-e color sensibility (without imitation)
- washi paper aesthetics
- Montessori calm learning design
- the family's Japanese learning goals


## Prompt emphasis
Emphasize quiet wonder, study as affection, and visual serenity.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- anime exaggeration
- busy collage overload
- neon saturation
- gag humor that breaks calm


## Good blend partners

- 05 Montessori Meadow Cut-Paper
- 08 Piano Rain Geometry
- 01 Constitution base


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?
