# Maker Constellation — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Ideas become systems you can see. Diagrams, constellations, tools, and wonder share one visual grammar.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The intellectual identity style: systems, tools, cosmos, and hopeful making.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- STEM content
- maker education
- innovation posters
- agentic/digital explainers for family audiences
- project branding


## Core metaphors

- constellations
- blueprints
- telescopes
- notebooks
- tools
- orbital paths


## Material language

- storybook illustration with diagram overlays
- chalkboard and blueprint accents
- soft glows
- wood-and-metal workshop texture


## Palette cues

- atlas blue
- starlight gold
- timber oak
- orbit lilac
- sage green
- blueprint cream


## Composition rules

- mix narrative scenes with explanatory notation
- integrate tools naturally
- use celestial arcs to connect ideas
- make complexity feel inviting
- balance wonder with legibility


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- observatory charts
- maker culture
- scientific illustration
- informational children's books
- the user's systems/maker identity


## Prompt emphasis
Make intelligence feel warm and shareable, not cold or elite.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- corporate sterility
- overly technical abstraction
- grim dystopian tech
- visual clutter that kills legibility


## Good blend partners

- 09 Wonder Workshop Toymaker
- 07 Lenslight Field Journal
- 10 Hearthlight Future Garden


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Maker Constellation within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.
Mood: warm, curious, hopeful, emotionally safe.
Narrative goal: [insert goal].
Use motifs such as: constellations, blueprints, telescopes, notebooks, tools.
Visual language should emphasize: storybook illustration with diagram overlays, chalkboard and blueprint accents, soft glows.
```
