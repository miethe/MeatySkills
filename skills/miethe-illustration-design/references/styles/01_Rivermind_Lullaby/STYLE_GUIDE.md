# Rivermind Lullaby — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Thoughts become landscape. Feelings are rivers, moonlight, lanterns, and quiet water.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The emotional hearth: bedtime calm, reassurance, tenderness, and the river-of-thought metaphor.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- bedtime stories
- sleep and calm content
- emotional literacy
- family rituals
- gentle audio/visual experiences


## Core metaphors

- rivers
- moon
- lanterns
- pine forests
- quiet cabins
- starlit guidance


## Material language

- watercolor washes
- soft gouache texture
- quilt and knit patterning
- glowing light blooms


## Palette cues

- midnight blue
- lake silver
- lantern gold
- pajama lilac
- soft cream
- pine green


## Composition rules

- close family grouping
- soft curved lines
- foreground comfort objects
- wide calm negative space in sky/water
- light source as emotional anchor


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- lyrical bedtime picture books
- Scandinavian hygge illustration
- Appalachian lakeside atmosphere
- quiet cinematic night scenes
- the user's bedtime river metaphor


## Prompt emphasis
Emphasize safety, closeness, slowing down, and a gentle transition from wakefulness into peace.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- chaotic composition
- loud saturation
- hyperactive poses
- ironic tone
- hard sci-fi aesthetics


## Good blend partners

- 08 Piano Rain Geometry
- 02 Blue Ridge Campfire Quilt
- 01 Constitution base


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Rivermind Lullaby within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.

Narrative goal: [insert goal].

Visual language should emphasize: watercolor washes, soft gouache texture, quilt and knit patterning.
```
