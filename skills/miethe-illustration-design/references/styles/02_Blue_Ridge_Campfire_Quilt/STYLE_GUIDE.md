# Blue Ridge Campfire Quilt — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Family memory becomes textile folklore. Life is stitched from adventures, seasons, and keepsakes.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The regional memory style: home, mountain belonging, camping, and generational warmth.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- family adventure stories
- travel keepsakes
- regional prints
- outdoor learning
- seasonal wall art


## Core metaphors

- quilts
- campfires
- mountain ridges
- stitched seams
- trail signs
- heirloom objects


## Material language

- quilt texture
- folk-art simplification
- woodgrain accents
- cozy painterly firelight


## Palette cues

- indigo
- campfire amber
- mountain blue
- moss green
- cream
- berry rose


## Composition rules

- patchwork framing devices
- warm central glow
- landscape depth
- layered memorabilia
- objects used as memory anchors


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- Appalachian craft traditions
- American folk quilts
- family camping imagery
- Blue Ridge landscape painting
- cozy outdoor editorial illustration


## Prompt emphasis
Emphasize North Carolina mountain identity, family togetherness, and memory-making through outdoors experiences.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- sterile modern minimalism
- urban sleekness
- grim wilderness realism
- lonely compositions


## Good blend partners

- 07 Lenslight Field Journal
- 01 Rivermind Lullaby
- 06 Mosaic Story Atlas


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Blue Ridge Campfire Quilt within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.
Mood: warm, curious, hopeful, emotionally safe.
Narrative goal: [insert goal].
Use motifs such as: quilts, campfires, mountain ridges, stitched seams, trail signs.
Visual language should emphasize: quilt texture, folk-art simplification, woodgrain accents.
```
