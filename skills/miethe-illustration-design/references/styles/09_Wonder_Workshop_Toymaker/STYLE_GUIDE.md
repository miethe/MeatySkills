# Wonder Workshop Toymaker — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Ideas become things. Play and invention share one tabletop.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The playful construction style: building, tinkering, and imaginative objects with heart.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- DIY projects
- kids maker kits
- craft apps
- toy-rich storytelling
- playful product concepts


## Core metaphors

- wooden robots
- blocks
- gears
- planes
- workbenches
- prototype sketches


## Material language

- warm workshop wood
- toy-like form language
- friendly robotics
- paper plans and parts


## Palette cues

- workshop tan
- robot blue
- tool red
- honey amber
- sage
- blueprint cream


## Composition rules

- busy but legible tabletop scenes
- friendly modular forms
- objects feel buildable
- play is structured by curiosity and kindness


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- Waldorf/Montessori making culture
- wooden toy design
- maker spaces
- friendly robotics
- the user's 3D printing/woodworking/building interests


## Prompt emphasis
Convey agency, play, making, and invention as family culture.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- militarized robots
- cold industrial fabrication
- mess without purpose


## Good blend partners

- 04 Maker Constellation
- 05 Montessori Meadow Cut-Paper
- 10 Hearthlight Future Garden


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Wonder Workshop Toymaker within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.

Narrative goal: [insert goal].

Visual language should emphasize: warm workshop wood, toy-like form language, friendly robotics.
```
