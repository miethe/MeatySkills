# Hearthlight Future Garden — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
The future is cultivated. Technology, home, family, and ecology are one living system.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The world-building style: hopeful futures, biophilic technology, and the civilization we want to grow.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- future-of-home stories
- optimistic technology visuals
- sustainability education
- AI-with-heart concepts
- vision pieces


## Core metaphors

- gardens
- terrariums
- sunrise
- clean water
- helpful AI companions
- living infrastructure


## Material language

- lush biophilic rendering
- soft futuristic glow
- glass, wood, and foliage harmony
- gentle holographic accents


## Palette cues

- hearth glow
- leaf green
- sunrise gold
- water blue
- soft white
- flower blush


## Composition rules

- family stays central, tech stays servant
- use abundance and light
- tie interiors to landscapes/cities
- make the future feel reachable and humane


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- solarpunk
- biophilic architecture
- storybook futurism
- family-centered sustainability graphics
- the user's future-building worldview


## Prompt emphasis
Show a better future being actively nurtured by real people in loving relationship.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- dystopia
- sterile chrome futurism
- dehumanized tech worship
- grim cyberpunk


## Good blend partners

- 04 Maker Constellation
- 06 Mosaic Story Atlas
- 09 Wonder Workshop Toymaker


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Hearthlight Future Garden within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.
Mood: warm, curious, hopeful, emotionally safe.
Narrative goal: [insert goal].
Use motifs such as: gardens, terrariums, sunrise, clean water, helpful AI companions.
Visual language should emphasize: lush biophilic rendering, soft futuristic glow, glass, wood, and foliage harmony.
```
