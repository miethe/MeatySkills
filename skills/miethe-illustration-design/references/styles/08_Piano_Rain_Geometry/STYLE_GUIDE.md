# Piano Rain Geometry — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
Emotion becomes rhythm and geometry. Sound is rendered as motion, rain, keys, waves, and pattern.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The music-and-pattern style: emotion translated into rhythm, line, and gentle abstraction.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- music learning visuals
- poetic interludes
- motion-rich graphics
- emotion mapping
- meditative scenes


## Core metaphors

- piano keys
- raindrops
- waveforms
- notation
- echo lines
- harmonic arcs


## Material language

- translucent overlays
- musical linework
- soft rain texture
- glowing note motifs


## Palette cues

- rain blue
- midnight indigo
- silver
- pearl cream
- lavender mist
- warm glow


## Composition rules

- use repetition and interval
- allow abstraction to coexist with tenderness
- let rhythm guide eye movement
- maintain calm despite dynamism


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- music notation
- synesthetic visual art
- editorial abstraction
- lyrical children's illustration
- the user's self-taught piano interest


## Prompt emphasis
Translate feeling into pattern without losing warmth or readability.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- random abstract chaos
- club/nightlife energy
- harsh contrast


## Good blend partners

- 01 Rivermind Lullaby
- 03 Quiet Kyoto Playbook
- 04 Maker Constellation


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?

## Starter prompt
```text
Create a [medium] in the style of Piano Rain Geometry within the Miethe Illustration Constitution.
Feature Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, and Penelope.
Exclude dogs unless explicitly requested.
Mood: warm, curious, hopeful, emotionally safe.
Narrative goal: [insert goal].
Use motifs such as: piano keys, raindrops, waveforms, notation, echo lines.
Visual language should emphasize: translucent overlays, musical linework, soft rain texture.
```
