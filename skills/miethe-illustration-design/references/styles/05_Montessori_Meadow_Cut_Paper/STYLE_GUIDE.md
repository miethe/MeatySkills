# Montessori Meadow Cut-Paper — Agentic Style Guide

## Essence
The world becomes touchable pieces. Shapes, layers, and tactile objects invite understanding.

## Role inside the Miethe system
The early-childhood tactile style: hands-on discovery, simple forms, and nature-first play.

## Canonical cast for this style
Default to:
- Nick
- Wife / Mom
- Peyton
- Parker
- Penelope

Default exclusion:
- dogs unless explicitly requested

## When to use this style

- toddler materials
- early learning books
- shape and nature concepts
- simple activity cards
- gentle app mini-games


## Core metaphors

- paper petals
- leaves
- stones
- simple creatures
- movable parts
- sorting and arranging


## Material language

- cut paper collage
- felt/craft paper edges
- flat shape language
- soft shadows


## Palette cues

- meadow green
- flower pink
- sun cream
- sky blue
- warm coral
- soft lavender


## Composition rules

- big readable shapes
- clear silhouette hierarchy
- low cognitive load
- generous spacing
- everything looks touchable


## Family-specific guidance
- Make the family feel specific and lived-in.
- Nick should read as a calm, thoughtful builder/observer.
- Wife/Mom should read as a full co-anchor, not background support.
- Peyton should feel imaginative and observant.
- Parker should feel hands-on and curious.
- Penelope should feel bright, affectionate, and wonder-led.
- Preserve emotional safety, age-appropriate behavior, and warmth.

## Artistic influences and inspirations

- Montessori materials
- Eric Carle-like collage logic (without imitation)
- paper craft design
- nature tables
- preschool visual pedagogy


## Prompt emphasis
Keep it calm, tactile, inviting, and age-appropriate.

## Avoid / anti-patterns

- fussy detail overload
- dark moody drama
- sharp harsh gradients
- tiny unreadable objects


## Good blend partners

- 03 Quiet Kyoto Playbook
- 07 Lenslight Field Journal
- 01 Constitution base


## Agent checklist
- Is the selected dialect unmistakable?
- Does it still feel like the Miethe Constitution?
- Is the family present in a believable, affectionate way?
- Is Mom/Wife included when the composition warrants the full family?
- Are dogs excluded unless explicitly requested?
- Is the piece beautiful **and** useful?
