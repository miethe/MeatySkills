# Artifact Recipes

## 1. Children’s book cover
- Choose one primary dialect.
- Decide the emotional promise.
- Use one strong scene.
- Keep 1 title, 1 subtitle, and 1 small note maximum.

## 2. Two-page story spread
- Use one story beat only.
- Let background motifs carry subtext.
- Keep facial expressions gentle and readable.
- Ensure the spread remains low-overstimulation.

## 3. Educational card / vocabulary card
- One concept per card.
- Strong object or scene focus.
- Keep text extremely legible.
- Works especially well in Quiet Kyoto, Montessori Meadow, Lenslight.

## 4. App mockup
- Treat illustration as product identity, not wallpaper.
- Prioritize hierarchy and readable controls.
- Use style motifs in cards, headers, and thumbnails.

## 5. Family keepsake print
- Emphasize place, memory, or shared values.
- Use 1-2 handwritten notes.
- Suitable dialects: Blue Ridge, Mosaic, Rivermind, Hearthlight.

## 6. Maker / STEM graphic
- Combine narrative scene with instructional logic.
- Suitable dialects: Maker Constellation, Wonder Workshop, Lenslight.

## 7. Future world / optimistic vision piece
- Put family or people first.
- Let technology remain humane and secondary.
- Best dialect: Hearthlight Future Garden.
