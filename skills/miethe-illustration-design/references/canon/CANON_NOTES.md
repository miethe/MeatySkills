# Canon Notes

## Canon vs exploratory archive
The pack includes archive imagery created during exploration.
Those images remain valuable references, but they do not override current canon.

## Current canon
- Family cast: Nick, Wife/Mom, Peyton, Parker, Penelope
- Default animal presence: none
- Dogs: excluded unless explicitly requested
- Tone: warm, hopeful, specific, human
- Function: usable across children’s books, apps, posters, cards, storyboards, and future experiments

## When archive images conflict with canon
1. Keep the mood and structural lessons.
2. Update the cast to current canon.
3. Remove dogs unless the brief says otherwise.
4. Preserve the chosen style dialect.
