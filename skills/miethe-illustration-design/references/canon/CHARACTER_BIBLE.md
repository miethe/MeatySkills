# Character Bible

## Canonical family cast
### Nick
- Adult father
- Warm, emotionally present, protective, intellectually curious
- Visual cues: brown wavy/curly hair, beard, kind eyes, outdoors-maker-professional hybrid energy
- Typical props: camera, journal, telescope, tools, books, backpack, notebook, mug

### Wife / Mom
- Adult mother / spouse
- Grounding co-anchor
- Warm, loving, practical, nurturing, capable
- Visual cues: gentle confidence, active/healthy energy, clinician-meets-home warmth, natural presence
- Typical props: books, tea/mug, running or outdoor layers, care objects, plants, learning materials

### Peyton
- Eldest daughter
- Imaginative, observant, expressive
- Visual cues: long brown hair, reflective expression, gentle wonder
- Typical props: camera, sketchbook, storybook, journal

### Parker
- Middle daughter
- Curious, builder-minded, connective thinker
- Visual cues: energetic curiosity, often curly brown hair, engaged hands-on posture
- Typical props: magnifying glass, blocks, tools, robots, binoculars, notebooks

### Penelope
- Youngest daughter
- Bright, affectionate, playful, wonder-filled
- Visual cues: younger scale, light/blonde curls, delighted or cuddly affect
- Typical props: plush comfort object, flowers, cards, small treasures, simple blocks

## Relationship rules
- Family closeness should feel natural and lived-in.
- Nick and Wife/Mom are co-anchors, not one-dimensional archetypes.
- The children must each remain distinct.
- No one should feel like a stock placeholder.
- Use warmth and presence over perfection.

## Archive note
Historic exploratory images in this pack often include dogs; do not treat them as part of the current canonical cast unless specifically requested.
