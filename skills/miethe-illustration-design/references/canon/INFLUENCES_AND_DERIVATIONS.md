# Influences and Derivations

This system is intentionally **influenced by many real-world visual languages** without aiming to directly copy any one artist or franchise.

## Recurrent influence families
- children’s storybook illustration
- educational editorial graphics
- scientific and observational drawing
- Scandinavian and Appalachian coziness
- Japanese restraint and seasonal calm
- Montessori material logic
- folk craft, quilting, and hand-made textures
- astronomy and notebook diagramming
- travel journaling and cartographic memory
- solarpunk / biophilic hopeful futures

## Important derivation rule
Influence is used at the level of:
- material language
- compositional logic
- emotional register
- motif systems
- palette and light logic

Influence is **not** meant to imply direct imitation of a single living artist or copyrighted visual property.
