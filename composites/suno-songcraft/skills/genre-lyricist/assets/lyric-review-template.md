## Changes made

Record only substantive changes. For a disputed or melody-sensitive line, preserve the original and supply a separate alternative.

## Uncertainties

State whether the melody/audio was actually inspected. Name the precise phrase or issue that needs a performance test. Do not equate equal syllable counts with proven melody fit.
