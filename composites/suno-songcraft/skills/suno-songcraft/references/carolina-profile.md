# Optional profile: Carolina Rain

Use only for this song or an explicitly requested related palette. This is a user-specific creative example, not a global default for the skill.

## Protected identity

A rural Carolina homecoming/redemption song from a narrator with a 1990s upbringing. Keep soulful clean blues-funk guitar recognizable while adding raw roots texture. The chorus is the song's anchor; do not replace it with generic nostalgia.

## Arrangement intent

- One low, raspy male frontman and one rich, smoky female co-lead. They trade substantial solo blocks and join in harmony.
- A male backing trio supports the pair; no unrequested third lead. Alternative-rock grit colors selected harmony peaks rather than taking over the melody.
- Slow 12/8 at a target 78 BPM. Treat the intended counted beat as an assumption pending the source recording; retain its actual groove when audio is available.
- Clean single-coil guitar with muted syncopation, blues bends, and double-stops remains a sonic anchor.
- Acoustic guitar, sparse banjo, mandolin, fiddle, and dobro supply roots texture by turns. Woody bass, subtle organ, and drums bind the band together.
- Rawness comes from attack, breath, phrasing, live-room texture, and dirty accents, not only louder distortion or faster tempo.

## Lyric considerations

The supplied rain/washing image can support romantic reconciliation, spiritual overtones, or intentional ambiguity. Do not force a religious reading just because of the writer's identity. Show agency: choosing to stay, admitting fault, or offering comfort gives redemption an action.

Local detail should reveal the speaker, not decorate the setting. A screen door or creek bank can be an invented scene; do not label it Nick's actual childhood without his confirmation. A cassette detail can signal era without turning the song into a reference checklist.

The bridge works as reciprocal vulnerability: one lead admits difficulty, the other answers, then they reverse roles. Backings enter at the shared realization rather than narrating a new story.
